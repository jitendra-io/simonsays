ℹ️ How to Play

  1. Press Start to begin. Simon will light up one pad and play its tone.
  2. Memorise the sequence — it grows by one new pad every round.
  3. When the LED turns green (Your turn), repeat the sequence by tapping the pads in the exact same order.
  4. Finish the sequence correctly to advance to the next round, where one more step is added.
  5. Tap a wrong pad and the game ends. Beat your Best score to set a new record!

💡 Tip: Say the colours out loud as they appear — it's a proven memory trick. Sounds can be muted by your device's volume control.
